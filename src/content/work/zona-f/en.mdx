<CaseSection kind="challenge">

Betting sites have cluttered interfaces: hundreds of markets, banners everywhere and a bet slip that's hard to follow. I set out to design and build a prototype that keeps every feature of a real sportsbook, but organised so that placing a bet is clear and fast.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="solution">

<SolutionItem title="Always-visible bet slip">

that instantly calculates potential winnings, with quick stakes (+10, +50, x2).

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Accumulators">

with multiple selections on the same slip.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Live matches">

with the current minute, score and updated 1X2 odds.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Favourite leagues">

per user and a league sidebar with match counts.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Promotions">

(welcome bonus, cashback, free bet, accumulator boost, VIP) in a carousel.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Cash Out">

settle a bet before the match ends.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Responsible gambling">

with configurable limits, reachable from the menu.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Sign-in">

with email and password or with Google.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Mobile first">

on small screens the bet slip becomes a floating button.

</SolutionItem>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="outcome">

A complete, navigable product that shows how to solve the user experience of a complex application, with many states and data that changes in real time.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="developers">

<DevItem label="Stack">

**Next.js (App Router) + React 19**, server-rendered with Server Components and mutations via Server Actions.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Language and styles">

**Strict TypeScript**, no `any`. **Tailwind CSS v4** with brand colours as tokens.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="State">

**Zustand** for client state (bet slip, favourites, filters and live odds); slip and favourites persisted to `localStorage`.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Data and rules">

**Supabase (Postgres)**: business rules live in **SQL functions** (placing bets, accumulators, match settlement, promotions, responsible-gambling limits and Cash Out). Anything that moves money runs server-side, and data is protected with **RLS**.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Authentication">

**Supabase Auth** with `@supabase/ssr`: email and password plus Google OAuth; the session is refreshed on every request.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Simulation">

**Live match simulation** with custom scripts: moving odds, settling matches and seeding demo data.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Images">

**AI-generated images** (OpenAI) in a separate script; AI plays no part while the site is running.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Deployment">

**Vercel** with automatic deploys on every push to `main` · ESLint · `sharp`.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Code">

_Private repository: available to walk through in an interview._

</DevItem>

</CaseSection>
