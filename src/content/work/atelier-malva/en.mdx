<CaseSection kind="challenge">

A hair salon needs clients to book in a minute, without calling, and needs to manage its schedule without two appointments ever overlapping. I set out to do it with real-time availability and a database that prevents overlaps by itself, with privacy built in from day one.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="solution">

<SolutionItem title="Booking with no double bookings">

service, stylist, day and time, with the free slots shown live. The database prevents a stylist from having two overlapping appointments, even when two people book at the same moment.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Manage the appointment, with or without an account">

a personal link by email to view, change or cancel (only its fingerprint is stored), or an account with a magic link, no password.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Admin panel">

a weekly schedule by stylist, moving and cancelling appointments with an email notice, services, hours and holidays.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="A panel anyone can try">

with one click you enter a **read-only** account enforced by the database itself, and other people's data appears masked.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Privacy by design">

deletion and anonymisation on request, anti-abuse limits that store only fingerprints with an expiry, and pages that say what is stored.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Accessible and responsive">

WCAG AA level, controls of at least 44 px and an automated sweep of 18 widths between 320 and 1280 px.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Demo mode">

notices asking for fictional data in the forms, and a before-and-after gallery with AI-generated images, labelled as such.

</SolutionItem>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="outcome">

A complete booking website with tested security: 480 unit tests, 255 integration tests against the database and 1130 end-to-end tests. I built it with a specification, a plan, 44 tasks with their review and evidence, and 31 documented decisions.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="developers">

<DevItem label="Base">

**Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19**, with server actions.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Language and styles">

**Strict TypeScript**, no `any`. **Tailwind CSS v4** with design tokens and a contrast-checked palette.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Data and security">

**Supabase** (Postgres, Auth and Realtime) with row-level security on every table and SQL functions for the critical operations.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Validation">

**Zod** on all external input; types are derived from the schemas.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Testing">

**Vitest** and **Testing Library**; integration tests against a local Supabase; **Playwright** with **axe** for the full flow and accessibility.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Method">

Specification-driven development: spec, plan, 44 tasks with their review and 31 documented decisions (ADRs).

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Deployment">

**Vercel** and **Supabase**, with CI on GitHub Actions and a daily keep-alive.

</DevItem>

</DevItem>
