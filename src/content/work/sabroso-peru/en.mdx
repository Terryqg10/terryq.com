<CaseSection kind="challenge">

A restaurant needs visitors to see the menu and book a table without friction, most often from their phone. I set out to do it with no accounts, no database and no server of my own, with accessibility and privacy built in from day one.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="solution">

<SolutionItem title="Booking by WhatsApp">

a form with per-field validation that opens the restaurant's chat with the message already written. It says clearly that the booking is **not confirmed** until the restaurant replies.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Interactive menu">

with a category filter, tags (vegetarian, gluten-free, spicy) and the allergens declared on every dish.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Takeaway">

explains how to order by phone or WhatsApp, with no cart and no payment on the site.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Accessible by design">

WCAG 2.1 level AA, keyboard navigation, visible focus, controls of at least 44 px and support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="No data stored">

what the customer types never leaves their browser: no cookies and no analytics.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Content that gets validated">

the menu, the gallery and the reviews are checked at build time. A photo without alt text or a quote without a source and a date fails the build.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Legal footer and contact">

opening hours, address, and legal notice, privacy and accessibility pages.

</SolutionItem>

<SolutionItem title="Demo mode">

`noindex` and a `robots.txt` that block the site, so it is never mistaken for a real restaurant.

</SolutionItem>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="outcome">

A complete and fast site: in the latest measurement, Lighthouse scores 99–100 for performance and 100 for accessibility, with 85 unit tests and 154 end-to-end tests. I built it with a specification, a plan, tasks and documented decisions.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection kind="developers">

<DevItem label="Base">

**Next.js (App Router) + React 19**, with static pages.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Language and styles">

**Strict TypeScript**, no `any`. **Tailwind CSS v4** with design tokens: components use no loose colours or sizes.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Validation">

**Zod** on all incoming data: the form and the content. Types are derived from the schemas.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Testing">

**Vitest** and **Testing Library** for logic and components; **Playwright** with **axe** for the full walkthrough and accessibility.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Method">

Specification-driven development: spec, plan, 11 tasks with their review and 4 documented decisions (ADRs).

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Deployment">

**Vercel**, with CI on GitHub Actions.

</DevItem>

<DevItem label="Code">

Public repository: `github.com/Terryqg10/sabroso-peru`

</DevItem>

</CaseSection>
